“Knowing yourself is the beginning of all wisdom.”

— Aristotle

When I began my undergraduate journey in Computer Science, artificial intelligence felt like an infinite canvas. But taking Aristotle's reflection to heart prompted me to pause and examine what truly drives me. Looking inward helped me uncover two complementary forces that define my work: a clear sense of purpose rooted in community impact, and an intellectual fascination with the fundamental mechanics of General AI.

My sense of purpose comes from over ten years of volunteering and community teaching with the Associated Initiative for Mankind (AIM) Foundation. Working with underprivileged children in rural and low-bandwidth environments across West Bengal and Odisha grounded my technical outlook in reality. Technology cannot simply be an abstract exercise; it must solve real human problems, uplift underserved communities, and function reliably when people depend on it.

At the same time, my intellectual curiosity spans the core frontiers of General AI. I am deeply captivated by the challenges that stand between current models and truly dependable intelligence:

  • Hallucination Mitigation & Reliability: Developing statistical bounds and evaluation metrics so language models provide verifiable, calibrated outputs rather than confident errors.
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Engineering multi-hop retrieval pipelines and adaptive context re-ranking that resist retrieval noise and context distraction.
  • Model Alignment: Ensuring that autonomous systems stay safely bounded and aligned with human values and deterministic operational constraints.
  • Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Orchestration: Designing multi-agent systems that can plan, self-reflect, decompose complex workflows, and collaborate effectively in real-world environments.

My research trajectory is building towards this intersection: uniting the rigor of General AI research with deployment that genuinely benefits society. I am pursuing an MS in Computer Science in the United States to deepen this work alongside leading research groups.
